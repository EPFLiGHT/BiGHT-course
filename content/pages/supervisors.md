# Co-supervisors with open research projects

## Find a research project

LiGHT projects are **embedded in larger research programs** and divided into clear subcomponents. They can support semester projects, BSc and MSc theses, internships, and longer-term research through close, collaborative supervision.

**Choose a project direction that interests you, then mention the relevant supervisor(s) or project(s) in your application.**

Full-stack Software Engineer with a degree in Cyber Security (EPFL-ETHZ) but also Graphic Designer!

## Overview

**Block III: Trustworthy Evidence** (Weeks 9-13)

**Central question:** How do we know whether an intervention actually works?

**Big idea:** Moving from association to evidence about intervention effects.

**Block focus:** Design, evaluate, and deploy AI systems that are safe, effective, and ready for real-world use.

## Paired Lectures

Each week combines a domain lecture, an engineering lecture, and a project studio.

## Overview

**Block III: Trustworthy Evidence** (Weeks 9-13)

**Central question:** Why do effective interventions rarely reach those who need them?

**Big idea:** Implementation science and MLOps for global health AI at scale.

**Block focus:** Design, evaluate, and deploy AI systems that are safe, effective, and ready for real-world use.

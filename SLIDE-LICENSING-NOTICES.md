# Slide Deck Licensing Notices

Use these notices in BiGHT slide decks before publishing slides publicly on GitHub or the course website.

The phrase "except where otherwise indicated" is useful only if exceptions are actually and visibly indicated on the relevant slide or next to the relevant item.

## Compact Deck Notice

Use on a title slide, final slide, or credits slide:

> Except where otherwise indicated, original content in this presentation is © [year] [author(s)] and licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Third-party materials are excluded from this license and remain subject to their indicated rights.

## Very Short Footer

Use only where a footer is practical and exceptions are visibly marked elsewhere:

> Original content: © [year] [author(s)], CC BY 4.0, except where indicated.

## Individual Third-Party Item Notice

Use next to an image, figure, screenshot, quotation, map, icon, or other third-party item:

> [Item title/description] by [creator], from [source/link]. [License/rights status]. [Changes made, if any]. Not covered by this deck's CC BY 4.0 license unless stated here.

## Third-Party Material Under A Creative Commons License

> [Title] by [creator], [source/link], licensed under [CC license name and version] ([license link]). [Changes made, if any].

Example placeholder:

> [Title] by [creator], [source URL], licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Cropped and color-adjusted.

## Material Reproduced With Permission

> [Title/description] by [creator], from [source/link]. Reproduced with permission of [rights holder], [year]. Not covered by this deck's CC BY 4.0 license.

## Material Quoted Or Reproduced Under A Legal Exception

> [Title/description] by [creator], from [source/link]. Used here under [applicable exception or limitation, if identified]. Not covered by this deck's CC BY 4.0 license.

If you are not sure which exception applies, do not name one. Escalate the question or use a different item.

## Redistribution Rights Unclear

Do not include this item in the public version of the deck. Replace it, obtain permission, link to the original instead of reproducing it, or publish a separate version without the item.

Temporary internal note:

> Rights unclear for [title/description], [source/link]. Remove or replace before public release.

## Placeholders

- `[year]`: year of publication or release.
- `[author(s)]`: person or people licensing the original slide content.
- `[title/description]`: title if available; otherwise a clear description.
- `[creator]`: creator of the third-party item.
- `[source/link]`: source URL, DOI, book/paper citation, or repository link.
- `[license name and version]`: for example, `CC BY 4.0`, `CC BY-SA 4.0`, or another exact license.
- `[license link]`: URL to the license deed or legal terms.
- `[rights holder]`: person or organisation granting permission.
- `[changes made]`: for example, cropped, translated, color-adjusted, annotated, or no changes.
